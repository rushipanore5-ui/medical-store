07 · Business Improvement

Small steps, measurable progress

Simple steps to reach more customers and provide better service.

01
Complete online listings

Verify address, map pin, phone number, opening hours and holiday timings.

02
Make contact easy

Use a clear phone number, directions link and accessible store information.

03
Improve in-store experience

Keep shelves organised, signage readable and service respectful and accessible.

04
Request honest feedback

Invite all customers to share feedback; respond politely and never reveal private details.

05
Build a content routine

Plan two or three useful posts each week and check medical information before posting.

06
Review performance monthly

Track calls, direction requests, customer feedback and repeat visits where appropriate.

First 30 days

Set up

Correct business details, create branded templates and plan the first month's posts.

Days 31–60

Engage

Publish consistently, request honest feedback and respond to general enquiries promptly.

Days 61–90

Improve

Compare performance with the first month and repeat the content that helps local discovery.
